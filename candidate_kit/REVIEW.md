Also note anything you looked at and decided was **not** a defect, and why:

1. **`emp_code` is a plain string instead of MongoDB `ObjectId`**:
   - *Why not a defect:* The contract and `DATA_MODEL.md` explicitly state that `emp_code` is the business identifier assigned by customer HR (e.g. `EMP0001`), unique and immutable. MongoDB's `_id` is strictly internal and must never be exposed or used to identify employees in the API.
2. **`date` stored as a string `"YYYY-MM-DD"` instead of a BSON date**:
   - *Why not a defect:* Calendar dates are intentional plain strings in the data model. As stated in `DATA_MODEL.md`, a calendar date is not an instant, and storing it as a datetime forces an arbitrary time zone onto it.
3. **No endpoint creates `ABSENT` or `LEAVE` records**:
   - *Why not a defect:* The specification expressly confirms that `ABSENT` and `LEAVE` records originate from back-office seed data or manual regularization (`PATCH`), and that absence of a record is distinct from an `ABSENT` record.
4. **`history` and `half_day` missing in older documents**:
   - *Why not a defect:* `DATA_MODEL.md` Section 5 (Sample 9) documents that legacy documents may omit `history` and `half_day`. Missing `history` is treated as `[]` and missing `half_day` is treated as `false`.
